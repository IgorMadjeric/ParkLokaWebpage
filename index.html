Three simple steps

Park. Save. Return.

No account and no in-app map to manage. ParkLoka stays focused on the moment you leave your car and the moment you need it again.

01

Save your location

Tap once after parking. ParkLoka stores the coordinates and, when available, a readable address.

02

Add useful context

Take a photo or add a note, parking type, level, zone, and space so the last few metres are easier.

03

Find your way back

See the distance and an approximate walking time, then open the location in your preferred compatible navigation app.

Only what helps

A small app with a clear job.

ParkLoka keeps the information you need close without turning parking into another complicated workflow.

Address and coordinates

A readable address when the device can resolve one, with coordinates as a dependable reference.

Photo and parking details

Remember the entrance, level, zone, space, parking type, or any detail that makes the car easier to spot.

Distance at a glance

See your current distance, a rough walking-time estimate, and how long the car has been parked.

Your navigation app

ParkLoka hands the saved location to a compatible navigation tool you choose. Directions stay out of the app.

Share the parking spot

Send the saved location through Android’s share menu to family, friends, WhatsApp, or another compatible app.

Local-first parking data

Your saved location, note, parking details, and optional photo remain on your device until you remove them.

Private by design

Your parking spot is yours.

ParkLoka does not require an account or operate a server for your parking data. You stay in control of what is saved and when it is deleted.

Stored on your device

Coordinates, parking details, notes, and photos are kept in local app storage, not a ParkLoka cloud account.

Location only when needed

The app uses foreground location when you save a spot and while it is open to calculate the live distance back.

You choose external apps

Navigation and sharing open another app only when you ask. That app handles the information under its own privacy terms.
